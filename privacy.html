Privacy Policy

Angel AI Companion

Last updated: 20 November 2026

1. Introduction

Angel AI Companion is an AI-powered WhatsApp chatbot operated by Chizitere Nwankwo. This policy explains what information Angel processes and how it is used.

2. Information collected

Depending on your interaction with Angel, the service may process:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number.
  • Messages you send and replies generated by Angel.
  • Conversation timestamps and message counts.
  • Useful conversational information saved as memories to maintain context.

Please do not send passwords, payment details, or highly sensitive information to Angel.

3. How information is used

  • To receive and respond to WhatsApp messages.
  • To maintain conversation context.
  • To save useful conversational memories.
  • To operate, troubleshoot, and maintain the service.

4. Third-party services and processing

Angel uses the WhatsApp Cloud API provided by Meta to process WhatsApp communications. Message content and relevant conversation context are also sent to OpenAI's API to generate AI responses and periodically extract conversational memories.

Conversation records and saved memories are stored in a database on the operator's computer or server.

Meta and OpenAI process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Please review their policies for details about their handling of information.

5. Data storage and security

The operator manages the database used by Angel. Reasonable steps should be taken to protect the computer, server, database, and API credentials. No internet-connected service or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Data retention and deletion

Conversation records and saved memories may remain in Angel's local database until deleted.

To request deletion of information stored by Angel, send /forget to Angel or email soft23323@gmail.com.

The /forget command deletes locally stored messages, saved memories, and the contact record associated with your WhatsApp number from Angel's database.

This does not automatically delete information independently retained by Meta or OpenAI. Requests concerning those services may need to be made directly to the relevant provider.

7. Children's privacy

Angel is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to Angel, contact the operator.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Angel develops. Updated versions will be published on this page.
