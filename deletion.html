Your right to request deletion

Angel users can request deletion of information stored in Angel's own database.

Option 1: Request deletion through WhatsApp

Send this command to Angel:

/forget

Angel will delete the locally stored conversation messages, saved memories, and contact record associated with your WhatsApp number.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

Send an email to:

soft23323@gmail.com

Use the subject: Angel Data Deletion Request.

Include your WhatsApp phone number with its country code so the operator can identify the relevant record. Do not include your password, WhatsApp verification code, or other account credentials.

Important information

The deletion command applies to information stored in Angel's local database. It does not automatically delete information held independently by Meta or OpenAI.

For information held by those providers, you may need to contact them directly under their own privacy and data-request procedures.
